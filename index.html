01 / AI ENGINEERING

Data Anomaly Finder

CLAUDE AGENT SKILLS · PYTHON · DUCKDB · LANGFUSE

An AI agent skill that audits ESG data before reporting. A deterministic scan runs about 20 checks; the model reviews context, groups root causes and returns ranked, linked issues for sustainability managers.

72%
less peak scan memory
5×

Approach & results

On the 232k-answer benchmark, peak scan memory fell from 1,123 to 311 MiB and time from 11.0 to 2.1 seconds. Added a 1 GiB Docker benchmark harness and production feedback evaluation in Langfuse.

04 / RESEARCH

NPS & Product-Usage Study

PYTHON · SCIPY · STATSMODELS · METABASE · USERPILOT

An end-to-end study of what drives customer satisfaction, joining survey, usage, support and CRM data. Reproducible analysis tests about 30 hypotheses and publishes an automatically refreshed weekly dashboard.

12
sources joined
~30
hypotheses tested
Approach & results

Used customer-level analysis, bootstrap intervals and multiple-comparison correction. Found that usage explained less than 7% of customer NPS variation, and translated the evidence into six recommendations for customer-facing teams.
